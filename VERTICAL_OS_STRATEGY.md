# LensOS vertical operating system

## Destination

LensOS is the prompt-first operating system for photographers and videographers. One brief can coordinate the work from ingest through delivery, client communication, and business visibility.

## Why vertical wins

Horizontal tools solve isolated tasks and leave every handoff to the creator. LensOS follows the job vertically:

1. Ingest cards and drives locally.
2. Cull photographs or select video moments.
3. Hand approved work to Adobe with decisions intact.
4. Prepare and deliver galleries or cuts.
5. Keep client feedback and approvals with the job.
6. Show time, costs, revenue, and profit.

## Autonomy contract

LensOS handles repetitive coordination. The creator stays in control of creative choices, deletion, publishing, messages, exports, and delivery.

## Thirty-day V1 wedge

Start narrow: a fast local-first, keyboard-first desktop culling application for high-volume photographers. Earn the right to expand vertically after ingest, previewing, ratings, persistence, events, and export are reliable with 5,000–10,000 images.
