# What photographers subscribe to now (2025–2026)

Typical **stack tax** for a working pro: **~$50–150+/mo** once you combine cull + edit + gallery + business extras. Multiple logins. Files bounce. Clients still confused.

## Common stack (the tax)

| Layer | Tools people pay for | Ballpark |
|-------|----------------------|----------|
| **Ingest / cull** | Photo Mechanic (+), Aftershoot Select, FilterPixel, Narrative Select | ~$10–15/mo or $150–300 once for Mechanic |
| **Edit (craft)** | Adobe Photography (Lightroom + Photoshop), Capture One | Adobe Photo ~$10–12/mo; C1 ~$18/mo |
| **AI cull / edit** | Aftershoot Select / Edit / Complete, Imagen, Topaz | Aftershoot Complete often **~$45–55/mo**; retouch AI ~$7–20/mo or per-image |
| **Client delivery** | **Pixieset**, **ShootProof**, **Pic-Time**, **CloudSpot** | Often **~$8–30+/mo** + storage tiers |
| **Business** | HoneyBook, 17hats, Dubsado, QuickBooks | ~$15–50/mo |
| **Storage** | Backblaze, Dropbox, Google One | ~$6–12/mo |
| **Website** | Squarespace, Format, Pixieset site | ~$16–30/mo |

### Stack examples
- **Classic:** Photo Mechanic + Adobe Photo + Pixieset ≈ **$30–50/mo** + Mechanic license  
- **AI-heavy:** Aftershoot Complete (~$45–55/mo) + Pixieset + Adobe still in the mix  
- **Budget:** FilterPixel + Adobe LR + free gallery hacks ≈ **$25–40/mo**  

**Pain:** 3–6 subscriptions, 3–6 logins, ZIP chaos between apps, clients still email “where’s my gallery?”

## Who owns what job today

| Job | Incumbents | Lens V1 stance |
|-----|------------|----------------|
| **Cull / pick** | Aftershoot Select, Photo Mechanic, FilterPixel | **Lens owns** — keyboard cull, stacks, scores, auto-pick |
| **Deep edit** | Adobe LR/PS, Capture One, Aftershoot Edit | **Adobe keeps craft** — honest ZIP handoff, not a third editor |
| **Client send** | Pixieset, ShootProof, Pic-Time, CloudSpot | **Lens owns** — durable client gallery, PIN, favorites |
| **CRM / booking** | HoneyBook, 17hats, Dubsado | Out of V1 scope |

## Lens position (subscription strategy)
**One spine instead of three bills for cull + send:**

1. **Cull** — Aftershoot Select–class job (near-dupes, soft rejects, keepers)  
2. **Send** — Pixieset-class job (shareable gallery, PIN, client favorites)  
3. **Hand off to Adobe** for deep edit — don’t re-subscribe a third editor yet  

You **keep Adobe for craft**. You stop paying + context-switching for cull + gallery + “where did the ZIP go.”

Lens is **not** trying to replace Lightroom in V1. Winning move: own the **create vs business** war at the **cull + send** layer with non-bubble words: **Import · Pick · Adobe · Send**.
