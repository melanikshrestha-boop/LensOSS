# Who Lens is for (V1 wedges)

## 1. Wedding & event volume shooters

**Profile:** 20–40+ booked jobs per year (weddings, corporate events, parties).

**Wants:**
- Maximum **time savings** after every shoot (cull is the black hole)
- **Predictable costs** — not 4–6 overlapping subscriptions that creep up
- A **year pipeline** so capacity and delivery status are visible
- Client delivery that doesn’t require a second SaaS just for proofs

**Lens answer:**
- **Jobs** board + annual capacity meter (default 40)
- Import → keyboard/auto pick → Lightroom → client gallery on one spine
- Stack tax target: **Lens + Adobe Photo**, not Mechanic + Aftershoot + Pixieset + retouch

## 2. Lightroom-native editors (consistency bottleneck)

**Profile:** Already lives in Lightroom Classic or Lightroom cloud. Main pain is **consistent, high-quality editing** across many jobs — not pure selection volume.

**Wants:**
- Selects handed off cleanly into LR
- Same develop look year-round (preset / style discipline)
- Checklist for skin, contrast, WB consistency across ceremony/reception sets
- Cull and gallery handled elsewhere so LR stays the craft room

**Lens answer:**
- Dedicated **Lightroom** phase after Pick
- Keepers **ZIP** named per job
- **Selects CSV** + **edit brief** (preset name + consistency goal)
- Job-level `lrPreset` + `editBrief` saved for the whole year pipeline
- We do **not** pretend to replace Adobe develop — craft stays in LR

## Who we are not (yet)

- Pure hobbyists with 2 shoots/year (overkill pipeline)
- Studios that need full AI train-your-style retouch in-app (Aftershoot Edit territory — later)
- Clients who only need a free Dropbox dump (they’re not paying for craft)
